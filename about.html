About Suryaya

An independent advisory built on one belief: good energy decisions start with good information, not a sales pitch.

Founder story

Why we started this

Suryaya began after watching families and farmers across Telangana get three wildly different solar quotes for the same roof — with no way to tell which one was honest. We weren't trying to sell panels faster; we wanted a way to help people ask the right questions before they signed anything.

What started as a spreadsheet shared with neighbours became a small, independent advisory: a free calculator, a knowledge base written in plain language, and a network of installers we'd vouch for.
