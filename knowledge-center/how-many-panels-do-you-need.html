How many solar panels does your home actually need?

The honest answer starts with your electricity bill, not a sales brochure. Solar sizing works backwards from how much energy you actually use.

Step 1: Find your daily energy use

Take your average monthly bill and divide by your per-unit tariff (usually printed on the bill) to get monthly units consumed. Divide that by 30 for a daily average.

Step 2: Convert to system size

Most of India sees roughly 4 usable sun-hours per kW of installed capacity per day, though this varies by state and season. Dividing your daily units by that figure gives a rough capacity in kW — our Energy Calculator does this automatically.

Step 3: Check your roof space

A standard 540W panel needs roughly 2.6 to 2.8 square metres. A 3kW system typically needs 5–6 panels and about 16–18 square metres of shadow-free roof.

Step 4: Leave room to grow

If you expect an EV or added appliances soon, sizing slightly above your current need can avoid a second installation later — worth discussing with your installer at the site visit stage.
