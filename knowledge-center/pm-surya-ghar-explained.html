PM Surya Ghar scheme, explained simply

PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana is the central government's rooftop solar subsidy scheme for residential households. Here's what actually matters when you're deciding whether to apply.

Who qualifies

Residential households installing rooftop solar directly through empanelled vendors on the national portal are generally eligible — the scheme is not available for commercial or industrial connections.

What it covers

Support is structured as a capped subsidy per kW of installed capacity, up to a defined system size, credited after installation and inspection rather than deducted upfront in every case.

What it doesn't cover

It doesn't cover battery storage, structural roof reinforcement, or capacity beyond the scheme's residential cap — plan those costs separately.

How to apply, in short

Register on the national portal, get your feasibility approval, choose an empanelled installer, complete installation and net-meter registration, then submit for subsidy disbursal. It sounds like a lot of paperwork because it is — we help walk you through each step during your free consultation.
