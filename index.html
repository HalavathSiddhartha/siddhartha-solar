Our mission

Solar decisions shouldn't depend on which salesperson you meet first.

Most people's first encounter with solar is a sales pitch, not honest information. We built Suryaya to flip that: start with clear numbers and plain-language education, then connect you to vetted local installers only once you understand what you're buying.

Why trust comes first

Every recommendation on this site is generated from your actual electricity bill, roof type and location — not a fixed sales quota. We show subsidy eligibility, realistic payback periods and the trade-offs of each system size, before any conversation with an installer happens.

How we work

Three steps, no pressure.

1. Understand

Enter your bill and roof details into our Energy Calculator to see an honest estimate of system size, cost and savings in under a minute.

2. Compare

Browse the Knowledge Center to learn about panel types, inverters, batteries and net metering — so you can judge any quote you receive.

3. Decide

Book a free consultation. We'll walk through your Solar Savings Report and, only if you want, introduce you to vetted installers near you.
